# ShopBoss — Complete Command Reference
# @AiBizRepoBot
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# PASTE THIS BLOCK INTO BOTFATHER → /setcommands
# (BotFather accepts one command per line: command - description)
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

start - Welcome screen, reset session & onboarding
help - Full command list
menu - Open main menu keyboard
cancel - Cancel any active flow instantly
app - Open the Mini App dashboard
setup - Onboarding checklist (start here)

dashboard - Business snapshot (today + this month)
today - Today's revenue, profit & expenses
summary - Full business summary
report - Weekly & monthly report with margin %
alerts - Active business alerts (low stock, profit warnings)
analytics - Full analytics with expense breakdown

sale - Record a sale — guided or quick: /sale Rice 5 2500
sales - Recent sales list
revenue - Revenue report (today / week / month)
profit - Profit & margin breakdown

inventory - View all products and stock levels
product - Add a new product (guided 5-step flow)
stockin - Add incoming stock to a product
stockout - Remove stock from a product
lowstock - Products below minimum stock level
reorder - Reorder quantity suggestions
stockvalue - Total inventory valuation by cost price
deleteproduct - Remove a product from inventory
customers - Top customers by total spend

expense - Record a business expense (guided)
expenses - Expense list with category breakdown

order - Create a new customer order
orders - View all orders and status
delivery - View pending deliveries
track - Track an order by reference e.g. /track ORD-123456

staff - View all staff members
addstaff - Add a new staff member
payroll - Record a salary payment

suppliers - View all suppliers
addsupplier - Add a new supplier
purchases - View purchase history

insights - AI-generated business insights (uses your real data)
advice - AI business improvement advice
ask - Ask ShopBoss anything about your business
workflow - Industry-specific quick actions and tips

recordsale - Record a marketplace sale (guided)
mysales - View your marketplace sales (as buyer & seller)
confirm - Confirm a marketplace sale: /confirm 12 sent

profile - Your marketplace profile & rating
setname - Rename your business e.g. /setname Chima Stores
setindustry - Set your business industry (24 options)
setphone - Add phone number to marketplace profile
setlocation - Add location for proximity matching
setindustry - Change your industry

verify - Verify phone number (sends OTP)
otp - Submit OTP code e.g. /otp 123456

pay - Upgrade your plan (Monthly / Yearly / Lifetime)
refer - Get your referral link & stats
ticket - Open a support ticket e.g. /ticket My issue here
support - Contact support & view open tickets
settings - Business settings & account info
privacy - Privacy policy
terms - Terms of service
